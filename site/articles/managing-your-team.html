Team building

Managing your team

Documentation

Starting Lineup

Set your six court positions by dragging players from the bench onto the court. On mobile, tap a player and then tap a slot to place or swap them. All six positions must be filled before you can save.

Libero

Drop a player into the dedicated libero slot to use a libero. The libero is a defensive specialist who rotates in for a back-row player and cannot attack or serve.

Libero Replacements

When a libero is set, choose which front-row and back-row players they come in for as the lineup rotates.

Substitutions

Your coach automatically subs a tired starter for a fresh bench player once they reach the tiredness band you set. Choose a team-wide default in the Substitutions tab, or map a specific bench player to each starter with their own threshold. You can also mark a bench player as a pinch server who comes on just to serve.

Saving Changes

Your tactics only take effect once you press Save. Use Reset to discard any unsaved changes.
