Team Rankings

Documentation

A team's ranking is its rating, a FIVB-style Elo. Every competitive match updates both teams by the same amount: the winner gains exactly what the loser loses. Friendlies do not count.

The formula

After each match a team's rating changes by:

ΔR = (S - E) × W / 8 ΔR the change in your rating S your set score value (how decisively you won or lost) E the expected set score value, from the rating gap W the competition weight

So beating a stronger team gains more than beating a weaker one, and losing to a weaker team costs more.

Competition weight (W)

Bigger competitions move the rating more:

League: 20 (promotion play-offs count as league) National Cup: 30 Regional Cup: 40 WCC: 50

The weight is halved for each bot team in the match, so beating other managers is worth more.

Set score value (S)

How decisively the match ended. The loser gets the negative of the same value:

3-0: ±2 3-1: ±1.5 3-2: ±1 Expected result (E)

The expected set score value comes from the rating gap between the two teams. First the gap is scaled:

D = 8 × (R_you - R_opp) / 1000

That gap D shifts a bell curve, split by fixed cut-points (-1.060, -0.394, 0, 0.394, 1.060), across the six possible set scores. Each score's probability is multiplied by its set score value and summed:

E = Σ P(score) × value(score)

The closer the two ratings, the closer the expected result is to an even match.
