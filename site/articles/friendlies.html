Friendlies

Documentation

What is a friendly?

A friendly is an exhibition match. It has no effect on your team rating or any competition standings.

When are they played?

Friendlies are played every weekend, once the weekend cups begin. Your team plays eight of them: four on Saturday and four on Sunday, starting at 20:00 UTC. Every team plays, whether or not it is in a cup that weekend.

How are matches determined?

Teams are ranked by rating and split into groups of nine. You play every other team in your group once, which gives you your eight matches, so your opponents are always close to your team in rating. If a group cannot be filled with user teams, a few bot teams are added to round it out.

Rewards 150 on a win / 50 on a loss. Your players still gain training experience, but your team rating does not change.
