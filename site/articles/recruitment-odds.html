Recruitment odds

Documentation

How recruitment works

Each recruitment costs 100 (single) or 1,000 (×10). The player you receive is determined by weighted random chance based on the rarity odds below. Your wishlist influences the roles and traits of RARE+ players.

Special players can only be obtained through Player Recruitment once your scout reaches EXPERT level.

×10 guarantee

Every ×10 recruitment guarantees at least 1 RARE or higher player, regardless of base odds.

Pity system

Each tracked rarity has a pity counter. As you recruit without receiving that rarity, the counter rises and its odds increase a little with each recruit, with a guaranteed drop once the counter reaches the threshold.

Legendary: guaranteed after 25 recruits Mythic: guaranteed after 250 recruits

Receiving a higher rarity also resets the lower counters, since it already beats what you were building toward: a Mythic (or Special) resets your Legendary counter, and a Special resets your Mythic counter.
