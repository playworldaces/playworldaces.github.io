Season standings

Documentation

How are standings calculated?

Each team earns points according to the results of their matches:

3 Points: For winning 3-0 or 3-1. 2 Points: For winning 3-2. 1 Point: For losing 2-3. 0 Points: For losing 0-3 or 1-3. Tiebreakers

Each team has a set and points ratio (ratio between won and lost).

If two teams are tied on wins and points, the team with the higher set ratio is considered better. If tied on set ratio, then the points ratio is the deciding factor. If still tied, the priority goes to the team that won the match between both. Rewards

Per match rewards

150 on win / 50 on loss in Division 1; each division below pays 15 / 5 less.

Season placement

The season reward depends on the division and final position:

Division 1

Top 12: 1000 Top 8: 1500 Top 4: 2000 Top 3: 2250 Top 2: 2500 Champion: 3000

Division 2

Top 8: 500 Top 4: 1000 Top 2: 1250 Champion: 1500

Division 3 and lower

Top 4: 500 Top 2: 750 Champion: 1000 Promotions and relegations Every division except the top one has 2 promotion spots: 1st place is promoted automatically, and 2nd place plays a promotion playoff. Every division except the lowest has 4 relegation spots: 15th and 16th are relegated automatically (each to one of the two divisions below), and 13th and 14th play relegation playoffs. Promotion playoffs are played on Sundays at 12:00 UTC: a lower division's playoff team faces an upper division's, and if the lower team wins, the two swap divisions. Promoting earns 500 , automatically or by winning the playoff. Qualifying to the Regional Cup

The top 4 teams of the 1st division in their league qualify to their region's Regional Cup for that season. They compete against the top teams from the other countries in their region to see who is the best and qualify to the World Champions Circuit!
