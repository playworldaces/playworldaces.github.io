National teams

World Nations League

Documentation

Format

The 20 national teams, one per country, play a double round robin: 38 matches each, 380 in total. The top 8 qualify for the WNL finals.

Schedule

The WNL runs in editions of two weeks: a callup week, when national team managers select their squads, followed by a games week when every match is played. Points and tiebreakers follow the same rules as league play.

Rewards and consequences

Playing in the WNL gives no coins or recruit rewards to any user. Users will be mentioned in their national team's accolades page. Players will track their international performances in their stats.

Players are not affected by injuries in the WNL. They can be or get hurt during their club games, but that won't affect them in WNL matches. Dismissed players that are in a national team remain available to the national team until the end of the competition.
