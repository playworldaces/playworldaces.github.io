World Champions Circuit

Documentation

What is it?

The World Champions Circuit is an elite competition in which the best teams compete to win the ultimate glory, crowning them Champion among all Champions!

Who can play?

The top 3 teams from each Regional Cup qualify to play in the WCC. The final spot goes to the highest ranked team in the global rankings that has not already qualified.

When is it played?

WCC Matches start on Sunday at 12:00 pm UTC. Only one match is played at a time and they happen every 30 minutes.

How are matches determined?

The World Champions Circuit is a 16-team single-elimination knockout. The draw is seeded so teams from the same region are kept apart: two teams from the same region can only meet in the semifinals or the final, never earlier. Within that, each region's teams are spread across the bracket by their Regional Cup finish, and the regions are balanced by their average strength.

Rewards

Per match rewards

600 on win / 200 on loss.

Placement rewards

1st: 3× WCC Recruit Reward + 2,000 2nd: 2× WCC Recruit Reward + 1,500 3rd: 1× WCC Recruit Reward + 1,000 4th: 1,000
