Events

What is an Event?

Documentation

An event is a short side competition that runs alongside your league. Participating from events is optional, but has no downside. All events grant at least participation rewards.

Your event team

You never enter an event with your own team. The squad you draft belongs to the event alone, it cannot be moved to your real roster, and it is deleted once the event is over.

Events have special rules

There are no injuries or knocks in an event match. Event matches award no rating, no earnings and no career statistics, and your real players are never used, so nothing that happens in an event affects your in-game team or progress.

The events that run The War of the Commons: Common players only, over 7 days. Rare Gems: Rare players only, over 7 days. Stuff of Legends: Legendary players only, over 7 days. Age of Mythologies: Mythic players only, over 7 days. Super Special: Special players only, over 7 days.
