National teams

National Call-up

Official documentation

Format

Each national team calls up a final list of 30 players from its country's player pool. The shortlist holds up to 40 candidates in priority order; players move from the shortlist to the final list and back while the callup is open.

Deadline

Callups close on Saturday at 00:00 UTC. At the close the squad is finalized: open final list slots are filled from the shortlist in priority order, then topped up automatically with the best available players, so every team enters the games week with a complete squad. Teams without a manager receive a full automatic squad.

Who can be called-up?

Any player who has the nationality of your team can be called. No matter which league or region they play in. This view lists every single eligible player, but you can check on the top right of each player's card to see their nationality.

Injuries and dismissal

WNL matches are exempt from injuries or dismissal. Any player that is active will be available to be called, regardless of their injury status. Players can disappear due to dismissal, but once the deadline passes, rosters are set in stone. This means that any dismissal or retirement that affects a player in the final roster will not remove them from the national team until the end of the WNL season.

Players dismissed or retired before the deadline will not show up, that's why your shortlist allows for 10 backup players. Try to communicate with owners to see if they are planning to get rid of their players so you can make an informed decision. Use the community to plan ahead.
