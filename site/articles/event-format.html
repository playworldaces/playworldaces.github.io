Event format

Official documentation

Teams will be randomly sorted into groups. All teams in a group will play each other in a round robin while the event runs. On the final day, the top 24 teams will be placed in the final group and they will all play each other during that day. Teams are ranked the same way as in a league: first by matches won, then by match points, which are awarded in the same way as a league match: 3 points for winning 3-0 or 3-1, 2 points for winning 3-2, 1 point for losing 3-2 and 0 points for losing 3-0 or 3-1. If teams are still tied, the deciding factors are the set ratio, then the point ratio, then the result of the match between them. At the end of the event teams in the final group will be ranked in the same way and rewards will be granted.

How groups are formed

Once registration closes, the number of groups is set by how many teams entered. Groups are always equal in size, never smaller than 16 teams, and are padded with bot teams when the turnout does not divide evenly. Every tier sends exactly 24 teams to the final group.
