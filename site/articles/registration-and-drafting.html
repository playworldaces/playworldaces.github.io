Registration and Drafting

Official documentation

Every event is restricted to a single rarity. Every player you draft is of that rarity, and nothing else is eligible.

You can register while the registration window is open (details above). Registering means drafting a squad: you recruit 15 times, one at a time, picking one player from each selection of eligible players. The first 14 selections are each targeted at a position and offer 4 players, so together they fill a complete squad that can always field a legal lineup. The last selection is a free pick: it offers one player of every position, so you choose where to strengthen. The players you do not pick are discarded, and reopening a selection shows you the same players rather than new ones. If you quit or stop in the middle of the process, your draft progress is saved for you to continue later. If you do not finish the draft before the closing date, your registration is considered incomplete.
