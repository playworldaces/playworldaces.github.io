Lineup badges

Each player row shows a badge indicating their role in the current lineup.

Starter They occupy a court position. Libero They are set as the libero. Bench They are substitutes for each match. No badge - not part of the lineup. Age and decline

Each player ages one year per season. Once a player reaches their personal decline age, their stats will begin to drop each season. The status below their name reflects this:

Starting to decline - decline has begun, stats drop each season. Considering retirement - peak decline reached, no longer improving through training. Retiring a player

Players who are considering retirement can be retired using the Retire button on their row. Retiring removes the player from the active roster and adds them to your team's Hall of Fame, where their career stats are preserved. Players must be removed from the lineup before they can be retired.
