Team building

Roles

Official documentation

Setter

The architect of every play. Setters are at their sharpest when orchestrating the offense. They are general all-rounders that can benefit their team by buffing attacks and choosing the right targets and suffer little penalties for acting outside this role.

Libero

A back-court specialist built entirely around keeping the ball in play. Liberos are masters of reception and defense. A player with only this role will be badly suited to attack or block.

Outside Hitter

Outside Hitters are trained to do a bit of everything: attack, receive, defend. They live on the left flank and suffer little penalties for defensive actions.

Opposite Hitter

An explosive finisher on the right side. Opposites are built to swing hard and cause damage when the ball finds them. They perform the best when their only concern is scoring.

Middle Blocker

The defensive wall at the net. Middle Blockers are defined by their ability to read the opposing offense and shut it down. Don't expect them to do anything else well, though.
