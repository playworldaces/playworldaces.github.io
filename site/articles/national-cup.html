National Cup

Official documentation

What is it?

The National Cup is a single-elimination tournament that pits every team in a country against each other. The last team standing is crowned National Champion.

Who can play?

Every active team in the country's league enters automatically, across all divisions and tiers.

When is it played?

The National Cup is played on Saturdays. Round 1 kicks off at 9:00 am UTC, and every following round starts 30 minutes later.

How are matches determined?

The lowest division plays first. Its winners move up to face the division above, with pairings drawn at random each round so no two winners from the previous round meet. This repeats up the tiers until the top division enters and the remaining teams play out the final bracket.

Rewards

Per match rewards

300 on win / 100 on loss.

Placement rewards

1st: 2× National Recruit Reward + 2,000 2nd: 1× National Recruit Reward + 2,000 3rd & 4th: 1× National Recruit Reward + 1,500 5th-8th: 1,000
