Reference

SB

Official documentation

Two ways a team scores. Every rally is won either while serving (a breakpoint) or while receiving (a sideout).

Breakpoint %

How often a team wins the rally while it is serving. High values come from strong serving and defense.

Sideout %

How often a team wins the rally while the opponent is serving. It measures first-ball offense off the reception.

Modified Sideout %

Sideout %, but the free points from the opponent's service errors are removed from both the wins and the chances, so it reflects only the sideouts a team actually earned.

First Ball Side Out %

How often a team sides out on its very first attack after serve receive (a clean pass, set and kill), rather than winning the rally later in a longer exchange.

Modified First Ball Side Out %

The same, but measured over receptions that exclude the opponent's service errors.
