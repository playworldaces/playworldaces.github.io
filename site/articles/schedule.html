Schedule

Documentation

The schedule view will show you all your matches for the current event only. It will have information about all upcoming matches and all the matches already played.

When matches are played Every team in a group plays every other team in that group once. All groups play their round at the same time. Rounds are spread evenly across the days before the final, and always start on the hour or the half hour. The final group of 24 plays its whole round robin on the last day, one round per hour.
