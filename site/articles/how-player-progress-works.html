How player progress works

Documentation

Players earn progress points every time they play in a match. Once a player accumulates enough points, one of their stats automatically improves.

Progress Ring

Each ring shows how close a player is to their next improvement. When the ring fills, a stat boost is triggered automatically.

Thresholds by Rarity

Higher-rarity players have a higher stat ceiling, but they improve at the same rate as lower-rarity players. Each rarity has a minimum and maximum total stats that they can have. The closer they are to the minimum, the faster they improve. Improvement slows down the closer they get to the maximum. Age also plays a factor in the rate of improvement.

Stat Caps

Players of different rarities hit their peak at different times. The rarer the player, the higher the stat ceiling.

Coach Influence

The better your coach, the quicker your players will level up their stats. More experienced coaches need less matches to meaningfully influence their players.
