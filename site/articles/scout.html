Scout

Official documentation

Your scout is in charge of finding the best players for your team. The more you invest in their development, the better chances of them finding that hidden gem that will improve your team. Each upgrade reduces the chances of finding players and increases the chances of , , and who knows? Maybe, even, a player!

Scout levels LEVEL COST EFFECT AMATEUR — Base recruitment odds ROOKIE 3,000 / PROFESSIONAL 15,000 / EXPERT 40,000 / MASTER 80,000 / / Special unlock

From EXPERT onwards, Special players become possible to pull from a regular recruit.

Special recruits awarded as competition rewards are not affected by your Scout level.
