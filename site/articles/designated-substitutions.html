Designated substitutions

Documentation

Designated substitutions let you assign each starter a specific bench player and decide how and when they come on.

If a starter has no designated substitute, the coach brings in an undesignated bench player when that starter gets fatigued, and that substitute is governed by the team's general fatigue level.

For each starter you build the conditions that bring a substitute on, one of which is Tiredness. A designated sub with a bench can be a pinch server (comes in only to serve) or a regular sub that enters on your conditions and stays until your sub-back trigger brings the starter back.

Add a Tiredness condition to substitute the starter once their fatigue reaches the level you select, and combine it with score, rotation, or set conditions for finer control.

Each designated substitute has their own fatigue level, which starts at the team's general level. It sets when that substitute is replaced once they come on for the starter.

Note: if a starter is replaced in your lineup, their designated substitution is cleared and must be set again.
