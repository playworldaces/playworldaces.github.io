Players and staff

Traits

Official documentation

Master Mind

Has a chance to trigger on the team's first or second touch if the players involved in either of them have this trait. Grants a bonus to all the actions that follow that touch, until the ball crosses the net. Only one mastermind bonus can be active at the same time.

Moving Wall

When the player is in the front row and close to the attacker, they have a chance to join a block they would normally be too far away to reach. When it triggers and they join, the wall is stronger.

Surprise Attack

As a setter, dumps the second ball more often, and those dumps are harder to react to for blockers and diggers. As an attacker, it makes their spikes harder to block. When this trait triggers, the player will face at most one blocker.

Marksman

This trait can trigger on attacks and serves. When active, the action receives a light bonus and allows the player to find and target the place where the weakest defender is located.

Meteor Serve

When this trait triggers, the serve is a jump serve. It grants a bonus to its power and speed, making it harder to pass and slightly more likely to ace.

Vigorous

Has a chance on every action to perform it without spending energy.

Guardian

Has a chance to take over a serve-receive when the ball lands within their reach, stepping in for the player who would have passed it, even on a ball that would otherwise be an ace, and passing it with a bonus. If several players have it, the one with the highest reception takes over, and only one does. Hunter overrides it.

Float Serve

When it triggers, the serve floats with a bonus that makes it harder to read, and the receiver has to handle it overhand instead of with a bump.

Defensive Specialist

Has a chance, when digging a hard spike or putting up a block, to receive a bonus to that dig or block when evenly matched, or a better chance of success when the opponent is better. Does not apply to serve-receive.

Quick Learner

After three good receptions, digs, or sets in a row, the next reception, dig, or set cannot fail and comes out perfect. A poor or failed touch resets the count.

Momentum Addict

Starts with a penalty to their attack. Each attack made without an error raises their attacking power, and a kill raises it further, up to a cap. An attacking error resets it back to the starting penalty.

Loudmouth

Has a chance, when this player scores from the front row with a spike or a block, to rattle the opponents who faced them: all of their actions are weakened for the next 3 rallies. This penalty cannot stack but applying it again or multiple times resets the timer.

True Leader

Has a chance, when an on-court teammate makes an error on a serve, reception, set, or attack, to steady them: the teammate's next action cannot fail, and any active penalty on them is cleared.

Overachiever

Has a chance, when blocking, receiving, or digging an attack or serve stronger than they are, to rise to it and give their out-matched block, reception, or dig a real chance against it. Only available to Mythic-rarity players and below.

Crafty

Has a chance, on this player's serves and attacks, to make the opponent who follows their actions to spend extra energy. When this effect triggers, Vigorous cannot trigger.

Tenacious

If the team has lost three or more points in a row, every team mate receives a bonus to all of their actions, that gets higher the more points they lose. It disappears the moment the team wins a point. Multiple instances of Tenacious can't stack.

Hunter

Has a chance, when serving, to lock onto the opposing setter directly and force them to pass out of system, with a bonus to the serve that is greater against a front-row setter. Guardian cannot take the reception in their place. An overhand or float serve can reach the setter anywhere; a jump serve can only reach a back-row setter.

Last Resort

Has a chance, when a teammate's touch would otherwise lose the rally (a missed serve-receive, a failed set, or the team's own attack coming back off the block), for the libero to dig it up and send it over as a free ball, keeping the rally alive. Only possible when the dig would be a legal touch and the libero did not make the previous contact. Only a player in the Libero position with this trait can trigger this effect.
