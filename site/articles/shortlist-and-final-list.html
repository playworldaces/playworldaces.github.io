Shortlist and Final list

Documentation

Add players to your shortlist from the Players tab. Once on your shortlist, you can send players to the final list and remove them from there back to the shortlist. You can keep editing your final list until the deadline. Any slot left open will be filled with players from the shortlist.

You can order your players in the shortlist to establish a priority: the players at the top get sent to the final list if a slot is open by the deadline.

Players can be removed from the shortlist from the Players tab by clicking on the add/checkbox button again.
