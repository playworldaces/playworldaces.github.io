Wishlist

Official documentation

What is the Wishlist?

The wishlist lets you influence what kind of players you recruit. You can select up to 3 preferred roles and up to 3 preferred traits.

How does it affect pulls?

On every RARE or higher recruit, the wishlist activates:

40% chance the player's role matches one of your preferred roles. 20% chance one of the player's traits matches one of your preferred traits.

COMMON recruits are always fully random and are not affected by the wishlist.

Tips Fill all 3 role and 3 trait slots for the best results. Focus your wishlist on one playstyle or team need to get the most value. You can update your wishlist any time. Changes take effect on the next recruit.
