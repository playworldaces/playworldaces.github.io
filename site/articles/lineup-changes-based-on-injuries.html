Team building

Lineup changes based on injuries

Documentation

Knocks

During a match a player can pick up a knock, a blow that is not yet a long-term injury. A hard knock forces the player out of the match. A lighter one lets them play on hurt: their performance drops and every further action carries a higher risk of turning into a real injury.

Replace knocked controls what happens to a player who could play on: when on, they are pulled at the next dead ball; when off, they stay on court hurt. The setting belongs to the lineup slot, so a substitute follows the setting of the starter they came in for.

Replacing injured starters

If a starter has a long-term injury at match start, the replacement configured here takes their court slot and inherits all their settings. Starters without a configured replacement are auto-filled from the bench only. If no bench player is available the match is forfeited: reserves are never promoted automatically.

Bench backfill only applies when the replacement comes from the bench: the chosen reserve takes the vacated bench seat for that match.

Libero injuries

If your starting libero is injured before a match, choose who takes over: a reserve, or your second libero. Pick the second libero and you can name a reserve to become the new backup libero.

Single-libero in-match injury

If your libero goes down during a match, your second libero takes over automatically. With no second libero, you can name a bench player to be re-designated as the libero for the rest of the match.
