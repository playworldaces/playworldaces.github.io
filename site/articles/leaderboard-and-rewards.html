Leaderboard and Rewards

Official documentation

The leaderboard shows the global points for each team. Once the event ends, it will show your team's final standing and their corresponding reward. All rewards are cumulative, meaning that you will get all the rewards up to your final ranking.

Placement Reward Group stage 1x Event Recruit Reward + 1000 Final group 1000 Top 10 1x Event Recruit Reward Top 5 1000 Top 3 500 Winner 1x Event Recruit Reward Accolades

Your finish is kept on your team profile as a permanent accolade, shown as the band you reached. It survives the event data being deleted.
